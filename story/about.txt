ANCIENT TIGER: THE BLACK HOLE ESCAPE
Version 7.0.0

ABOUT THE GAME
Ancient Tiger: The Black Hole Escape is a mystical puzzle game

that combines ancient legends with cosmic phenomena.

Match colorful orbs before they reach the portal,

and prevent the ancient seal from breaking.

GAME FEATURES
🎮 Dynamic Gameplay

50 challenging levels with unique paths
Progressive difficulty system
Smooth 60 FPS gameplay
💥 Power-Ups

Bomb: Destroys nearby orbs
Slow: Freezes the chain
Reverse: Pushes orbs backward
Accuracy: Shows aim trajectory
🏆 Achievement System

36 achievements to unlock
Secret hidden achievements
Track your progress
🎨 Visual Experience

Beautiful particle effects
Dynamic black hole portal
Level-specific backgrounds
Smooth animations
🎵 Audio

Atmospheric background music
Satisfying sound effects
Adjustable audio settings
CONTROLS
🖱️ Mouse

Move: Aim shooter
Left Click: Fire orb
Right Click: Swap orbs
⌨️ Keyboard

ESC: Pause/Resume game
F11: Toggle fullscreen
TIPS & TRICKS
💡 Create combos for massive score multipliers

💡 Every 5000 points grants an extra life

💡 Use power-ups strategically in tough situations

💡 Watch for the danger warning - slow motion activates!

💡 Rainbow orbs match with any color
